Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how One Tap Escape ("the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

Information Collection and Use

One Tap Escape does not collect any personally identifiable information directly. However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob (for displaying ads and rewarded ads)

These third-party services may collect information such as:

  • Device information
  • Advertising ID
  • IP address
  • Usage data

You can learn more about how Google uses data here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Log Data

In case of an error while using the app, we may collect data and information (through third-party services) called Log Data. This may include your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, device name, operating system version, app configuration, and the time and date of usage.

Cookies

One Tap Escape does not use cookies directly. However, third-party services used by the app may use cookies or similar technologies to improve their services.

Children’s Information

One Tap Escape does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
