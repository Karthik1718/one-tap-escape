Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how One Tap Escape ("the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application.

Information Collection and Use

One Tap Escape does not collect any personally identifiable information directly. However, the app uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

Third-Party Services

The app uses trusted third-party services to support gameplay features, leaderboards, and advertising:

  • Google Play Games Services: Used for player sign-in, submitting high scores to global leaderboards, and unlocking achievements. Player profiles and leaderboard data are managed by Google.
  • Google AdMob: Used for serving non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.

These third-party services may process data such as:

  • Google Play Games Gamer ID & display name (for leaderboards and achievements)
  • Device identification & Advertising ID
  • Gameplay high scores and unlocked achievements
  • Basic IP address & network state

You can review Google's Privacy Policy to learn more about how Google handles user data:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Cookies

One Tap Escape does not use cookies directly. However, third-party services used by the app (such as Google AdMob) may use cookies or similar technologies to personalize ad content.

Children’s Information

One Tap Escape does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
